Installing on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android above, then open the downloaded file.
  2. Android will ask whether to allow installing from this source — tap Settings, turn it on, then come back. You only do this once.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. The app appears on the home screen like any other.
  4. The first time, the app asks for your activation code — see the contribution note above.
  5. That is it — it works with no internet from then on, on a plane, in the car, anywhere.

Why that permission step? The app is not on the Play Store, so Android treats it as coming from outside.

Tip for parents: the app starts by asking for the child's name out loud, and every instruction after that is spoken — so a child who cannot read yet can carry on alone. Stars and ticks start fresh each time the app is opened, so the certificate shows what the child did in that sitting.
